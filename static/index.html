How is your city doing right now?

Type a city to see its weather and 3-day forecast, air quality, recent earthquakes and disasters, crime rate, currency and vaccination coverage, gathered live from free public sources.

Data from Open-Meteo, USGS, NASA EONET, World Bank, UK Police, ExchangeRate-API and WHO. Readings are cached for up to a day per source.
